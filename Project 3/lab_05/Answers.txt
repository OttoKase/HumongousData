Q: One sentence: what does op="r" mean, and when does Debezium produce these events?
A: op="r" means that the event captured was from the initial read of the snapshot aka from where does Debezium start the "history" of the transaction. Basically all these r events are from the very beginning of tracking.

Q: why is Bronze always append-only — why don’t we UPDATE or DELETE rows here?
A: Because bronze is where all the raw information is stored so we can later on fully re-run the pipeline from scratch. It is basically the history of all changes to the db
